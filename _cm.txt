PRD persona-module grouping + state machines -- backend (Part 1/2)

Closes two structural gaps found when comparing NEXVORA's PRD output
against a real detailed external spec (Quran-memorization app PRD):
(1) content organized by persona/module (Student/Teacher/Admin-style)
instead of one flat list per section, (2) explicit state-machine
capture (e.g. Request Received -> Scheduled -> Live -> Evaluated ->
Passed/Failed) instead of burying transitions as prose inside a
business rule.
